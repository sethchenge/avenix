Our services

Each service below shows how we do it, the tools we use and what it costs. Prices are starting points and the final quote depends on the job.

OS installations

A fresh install fixes slow, infected or unfamiliar machines. We help you choose the right system, from everyday Windows to security-focused Linux, and set it up properly.

  1. Tell us about the machine and what you use it for.
  2. We back up your files and pick the right OS. Dual boot is fine too.
  3. Install and update. Drivers, updates and your must-have apps, such as VS Code, Git and a browser.
  4. Quick handover so you know where everything is.

Computer lab setup

We set up labs that are easy to manage. Every machine gets the same clean setup, so when one fails you can swap it in minutes.

  1. Site visit. We check the room, power and number of seats.
  2. Layout and cabling plan that you approve before we start.
  3. Install and clone. One standard image across every computer, plus the network.
  4. Train your teacher or admin and leave simple notes.

Machine maintenance and repair

Most slow or hot computers need a clean-up, not a replacement. We open it up, find the real problem and fix it.

  1. Diagnose. We test the disk, memory, battery and temperatures.
  2. Clean and re-paste. Dust out, fresh thermal paste.
  3. Repair or upgrade. SSDs, RAM, screens, keyboards, chargers.
  4. Test and return with a short note on what we found.

Robotics and Arduino

Students learn best by building something that moves. We start with a blinking LED and finish with a robot they programmed themselves.

  1. First circuit. LEDs, buttons and sensors.
  2. Make it move. Motors, drivers and a line-following robot.
  3. Add senses. Ultrasonic distance and Bluetooth control.
  4. Demo day. Each student shows what they built.

IoT solutions

Measure temperature, soil moisture or power use, and switch things on and off from your phone. We build it small and make it reliable.

  1. Choose what to measure or control.
  2. Build the device with ESP32 or Arduino and the right sensors.

Networking

Dead zones and slow Wi-Fi usually come from poor placement and setup. We survey first, then install and test.

  1. Site survey. We walk the space and mark problem areas.
  2. Cabling and hardware. Router, switches and access points.
  3. Configure. Secure Wi-Fi, a guest network and sensible limits.
  4. Test and document speeds and passwords for you.

RAM and hardware supply

Buying the wrong RAM is a common and costly mistake. Tell us your model and we supply and fit the right one. An online shop with delivery is coming soon.

  1. Send your model or a photo of the label.
  2. We check compatibility. DDR3, DDR4 or DDR5 and the maximum supported.
  3. Supply and fit. Same day where stock allows.
  4. Test that the full memory shows up.
