Our story

Avenix is a young company with a simple idea: when your computer, your network or your school lab is set up properly, everything else gets easier.

Under Seth Chenge, we install operating systems, build computer labs, repair machines, teach Arduino robotics and build websites and apps. We also make our own software. V Chess, our first app, helps chess players understand the engine instead of just copying it.

We are based in Nairobi and we work with schools, offices and families. We are small on purpose, so the person you talk to is the person who does the work.
